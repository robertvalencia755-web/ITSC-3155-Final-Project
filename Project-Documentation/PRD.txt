Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Product Vision Statement
Write your product vision statement here following the template from class or a similar template.


Key Terms (Optional)
Define a bulleted list of any key terms necessary to fully understand your problem context and solution.


Problem Context
Write a paragraph or two explaining the problem your software application aims to solve.


Key Hypothesis
Explain how your product will solve the problem in a paragraph or two.


Success Metrics
Explain how you will measure how successful your product is once you deploy it (eg.have x number of users sign up, make y process faster by z amount, etc).


Personas
Write a short description of at least two hypothetical users of your product and their needs.


Use Cases
Write a short description of how each of your personas will use your product. Write at least three use cases altogether using at least two of your personas.
