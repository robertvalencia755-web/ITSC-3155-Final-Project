ABOUT COURSE LENS

Making Course Discovery Easier.

CourseLens helps students explore courses, discover professors, and learn from the experiences of their fellow students.

OUR PURPOSE

Why We Built CourseLens

Choosing the right courses can be difficult. CourseLens brings course information and student perspectives together in one convenient place.

⌕

Course Discovery

Search for courses and quickly find the information you need to plan your schedule.

★

Student Reviews

Learn from student experiences and get a better idea of what to expect from a course.

✓

Plan Ahead

Keep useful course information in one place so you can make more informed decisions.

OUR GOAL

Plan With Confidence.

Our goal is to make course selection less stressful and more informed. By bringing together course information, professor details, and student reviews, CourseLens gives students the tools they need to plan ahead.

THE TEAM

Meet the CourseLens Team

CourseLens was created as a collaborative project by students working together to improve the course-planning experience.
